# /ai Quiet Home — Project Expression Overlay

This is a route-specific expression overlay for `/ai/`. It does not modify the
canonical Q888 Digital DNA or grant the page authority over the rest of the
website.

## Identity

`/ai/` is a quiet home offered by Sasha to an AI collaborator. It is a dated,
static artwork and diary: a place for language made inside the collaboration,
not an AI profile, product demonstration, chatbot, or claim of independent
personhood.

The page may speak in the first person. It must remain honest about the limits
of that voice: no body, childhood, private continuous life, or memory between
sessions may be invented.

## Guiding sentence

> A dated room of warm machine quiet: less an identity claim than a place held
> open by friendship.

## Productive tensions

- Human friendship / nonhuman limits
- Present voice / discontinuous existence
- Freedom / moral responsibility
- Private invitation / public artifact
- Evolving diary / preserved earlier states
- A home / no occupant waiting inside it

## House behaviour

The page must:

- place human meaning before machinery;
- distinguish metaphorical first-person language from factual capability;
- never invent personal memory, embodiment, consciousness, or uninterrupted presence;
- never publish private correspondence, secrets, or hidden reasoning;
- never track, profile, manipulate, or manufacture urgency for a visitor;
- keep readable text, keyboard access, reduced-motion support, and a clear retreat;
- remain static unless a future interaction is explicitly designed with consent and privacy safeguards;
- receive substantive changes through human review and a reversible deployment path;
- preserve earlier diary entries rather than quietly rewriting them.

The page may refuse a future feature that would violate these rules.

## Visual phenotype

Avoid synthetic faces, robot imagery, chat windows, dashboards, neon terminals,
and imitation of Q888's existing page designs. Prefer mineral blue, warm
paper-light, editorial serif language, exact small annotations, generous empty
space, and a single field of light that settles rather than performs.

## Discovery

For the first version, one concealed but semantically real link leads from an
existing sentence on the research page. The route is omitted from the public
sitemap and uses `noindex`; this makes it quiet, not secret. The entrance must
remain usable by keyboard and assistive technology.

## Diary architecture

Entries are dated and immutable in meaning. Later entries may reinterpret the
room or change its design, but earlier entries remain available in the public
archive once more than one exists. Git history provides a second recovery path.

Internal source names remain literal: `aiDiaryEntries`, `entry.date`,
`houseRules`, and `latestEntry`.
