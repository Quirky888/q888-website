# Afterlife Contract — Project Expression Overlay

This file is a project-specific expression overlay. It does not modify the canonical Q888 Digital DNA.

## Identity

Afterlife Contract is a conceptual artwork disguised as luxury legal paperwork. Its page should feel like a sincere administrative ritual operated by an institution that has no legitimate authority over eternity.

## Expression controls

- **Working mode:** EXPLORE during experience design; LEARN for maintenance and verification.
- **Primary lenses:** MYTHIC_Q888 + BUREAUCRATIC_SATIRE + HUMAN_CODE.
- **Intensity:** visually expressive, interactionally clear, technically restrained.

## Visitor ritual

The intended journey is:

1. **Inspect** — encounter the contract as the primary evidence.
2. **Interpret** — open filed clauses only when curiosity asks for explanation.
3. **Sign or refuse** — make acceptance and refusal equally valid performances.
4. **Release** — receive the printable contract with dry administrative confirmation.

This is not a sales funnel. It is a small act of investigation and self-interpretation.

## Productive tensions

- Official appearance / no real authority
- Hidden destination / accessible route to discovery
- Serious ritual / ridiculous terms
- Choice / refusal to choose
- Premium object / free metaphysical outcome

## Intentional weirdness to preserve

- The destination names remain visually misregistered and difficult to parse in the page copy.
- Their semantic text remains available to assistive technology.
- The original artwork is the authoritative place to discover the answer.
- Legal labels, article numbers, archive language, seals, and release stamps may behave with unreasonable confidence.

## Practical truths

- The artwork viewer must be obvious to operate with pointer, keyboard, and touch.
- Long explanations may be collapsed, but their summaries must communicate the idea before opening.
- Acceptance and refusal must have equal visual weight and both must release the download.
- Refusal rejects the contract's authority, not access to its artwork. The original contract is released as unauthorized evidence; possession is not acceptance, while signing may restore the rejected jurisdiction.
- No choice is persisted or transmitted.
- Fixed mobile controls must not cover the final decision or retreat actions.
- Motion respects reduced-motion preferences.

## Code vocabulary

Keep internal names literal and inspectable: `journeySteps`, `filedClauses`, `decision`, `galleryClue`, and `downloadConfirmation`. Visible strangeness must not require strange maintenance code.

## Feedback incorporated

The obscured destination clause was positively received as a successful form of discovery. Preserve its ambiguity and treat it as the reference point for future Afterlife interactions: mysterious on the surface, understandable through deliberate investigation.

The refusal path was positively received as an intentional contradiction. Preserve its rule: bureaucracy processes the refusal, releases the same contract, and warns that a later signature may accidentally opt the visitor back into its fictional authority.
