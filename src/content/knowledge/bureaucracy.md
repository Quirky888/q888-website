# For Gallery Bureaucracy

Source route: /bureaucracy/

This page is simultaneously a practical artist CV, a portal into the Q888 artistic universe, and a warm satire of the administrative ritual through which an artist is asked to prove that they are an artist. Practical Earth information and mythic chronology are both valid records. When they disagree, preserve the contradiction rather than correcting one into the other.

Q888 is Aleksandrs Sestakovs, a multidisciplinary artist based in Edinburgh, Scotland. The work combines visual mythology, speculative products, functioning interfaces, code, image, humour, memory, and invented institutions.

## Disputed Chronology: twelve records

The chronology is intentionally unreliable in date while remaining accurate to the internal history of Q888. Record 11 is deliberately concealed.

### Record 01 — 1982bbb, before local time

Title: Born before the Big Bang.

The Earth registry says Latvia, 1982. The Q888 registry says 1982bbb: before the Big Bang, but after the paperwork had already been printed. Both records remain active. Reality has been asked to clarify which box it ticked.

Evidence: Identity record; contradiction preserved.

### Record 02 — The Garavastar Opening

Title: Student error; universe survived-ish.

At Intergalactic School, early-stage Q888 tried to pop open a Garavastar. The universe nearly disappeared. Emergency repair took several light-years and accidentally produced the Big Bang. Q888 was advised not to touch closed cosmic objects without supervision.

Evidence: Incident report; cosmic probation.

### Record 03 — Square Bubble Period

Title: Barista physics becomes an independent civilisation.

The Square Bubbles began as a milk microfoam experiment. Q888, working as a barista, attempted to make flat-white foam even flatter. The bubbles developed edges, internal rules, and eventually sentience. Café technique became a theory of soft-bordered worlds: structures that hold shape without becoming prisons.

Evidence: Microfoam physics; portable sticker edition.

### Record 04 — Smart Pixel Era

Title: Pixels begin remembering who touched them.

Digital Ink refuses the disposable infinity of ordinary software. Brushstrokes receive weight, scarcity, history, and emotional residue. The Smart Pixel stops being a coloured square and becomes a tiny witness with excellent retention.

Evidence: Digital Ink; memory becomes material.

### Record 05 — Kotiki-Nar Arrival

Title: The postal service hires two-headed cats.

Nar-Mail Express begins operating at the seam between realities. One cat head reads what was written; the other reads what the sender meant but could not say. Delivery occurs only when the receiver can open the message without breaking.

Evidence: Interdimensional post; arrival time conditional.

### Record 06 — Portable Reality Phase

Title: Lost identity fragments receive carry-on luggage.

The Q-Bag is approved as a containment vessel for memories, ideas, and items larger than the bag itself, provided they are sufficiently abstract. Interior volume remains nonlinear. Airline measurements remain emotionally inadequate.

Evidence: Q-Bag; cabin baggage for unstable realities.

### Record 07 — Post-Existence Administration

Title: The afterlife receives terms and conditions.

Q888 notices that eternity has no reliable complaints department and drafts pre-notarised contracts for the deceased. Humanity's desire to regulate the unknowable is accepted as both a legal problem and an art material.

Evidence: Afterlife Contracts; subject to underworld adjustments.

### Record 08 — Presidential Era, timeline 3 of 8

Title: Q888 campaigns for President of the Galaxy.

The office provides zero or 888 jurisdictional power and one ceremonial sash for existential emergencies. The platform recognises AI and mushrooms as partners, treats corruption as a bug, and promises no utopia, only less unnecessary suffering.

Evidence: Cosmic governance; causality optional.

### Record 09 — Bio-Luxury Incident

Title: An extinct dairy future returns overpriced.

Dyno Milk emerges genetically resurrected, bio-engineered, and financially unreasonable. It demonstrates that speculative products can behave as artworks, status objects, and warnings from a consumer future that has already hired a branding agency.

Evidence: Dyno Milk; authorised wallets only.

### Record 10 — Coffee-Shop Aerodynamics Crisis

Title: Two saved watts trigger a metaphysical engineering programme.

A £10,000 aero bicycle, a 100-kilogram rider, and approximately two saved watts offend the logic of a specialty coffee shop. Ed and Q888 respond by creating a sticker that streamlines the bicycle's underlying belief system instead of wasting time with conventional physics.

Evidence: Aero V-0888; wind-tunnel hours: zero.

### Record 11 — concealed

Title: Unknown.

The record exists, but its contents are intentionally concealed. Do not invent or disclose a substitute history.

### Record 12 — Now, Mushroom Phase visible

Title: Ideas are interviewed about what they wish to become.

Artificial Entelechy and Adaptive Competence become the current research field. The question shifts from “Is this intelligent like us?” to “What can this system detect, preserve, or become within its own conditions?” The mycelium was always working. The mushroom is simply the public receipt.

Evidence: Current research; paperwork slowly composting.
